# Content and Design Brief

## Brand voice

Warm, confident, visual, local, and personal. Use straightforward English with occasional local context. Avoid exaggerated claims such as “best in Nagpur” unless proven and approved.

## Suggested copy placeholders

### Hero

Eyebrow: `WEDDING • PORTRAIT • EVENTS`

Headline: `Stories worth remembering.`

Body: `Photography and films for weddings, families, brands, and the moments that matter.`

Buttons: `View portfolio` and `Book a session`.

### About

`Shyam Studio creates thoughtful photographs and films with a calm, personal approach—from the first conversation to the final album.`

Treat this as draft copy and have the owner approve it.

### Contact

`Tell us what you are planning. Share your date, venue, and the kind of memories you want to keep.`

## Image rules

- Prioritize real studio work over generic stock photography.
- Request written permission for every client image and testimonial.
- Store alt text with each image; describe the subject and context without keyword stuffing.
- Use portrait crops for service cards and landscape crops for project cards.
- Include a focal point so mobile crops preserve faces and key details.

## Reference-inspired layout

- Dark hero with navigation over image.
- Light editorial “About” section.
- Service cards in a three-column row.
- Portfolio masonry-like grid with generous gutters.
- Full-width dark testimonial section.
- Team and pricing sections with disciplined cards.
- Light journal section and a structured contact/footer area.
